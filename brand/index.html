For press, promoters and partners: the Zentra logo, colours and type, and how to use them.

Logo

logo.png · 256 × 286 pixels, transparent background

Using the logo

  • Keep its shape and colours as they are.
  • Give it some space, on a dark or plain background.
  • Write the name as Zentra, and the company as Zenn Interactive Limited.

Colours

Type

Headings use Space Grotesk, the typeface of the Zentra app. Body text on this site uses DM Sans. Both are free from Google Fonts.

Describing Zentra

A short description you can use:

Zentra is a Nigerian platform for playing Chess, Whot, Lie, Ludo and Pool against real people, with a stake on each match. It runs in the browser at zentra.com.ng. 18+ only.

Please don't use the logo or name with promises of easy money, bonuses or guaranteed wins. Zentra doesn't offer them. Always say it's for adults 18 and over, and link to Play responsibly.
