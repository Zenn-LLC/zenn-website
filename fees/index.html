Money

Stakes and fees on Zentra Arena

Zentra Arena keeps 10% of what is staked in each match. Worked examples, the gift fee, the club fee and when you get a refund.

Zentra Arena makes its money from a fee on each match: 10% of what is staked. Every match shows what the winner takes before you join, so there are no surprises.

Match fee10%of everything staked
Stakes₦100–₦10,000per player
Gift fee10%player gets 90%
WithdrawalsNo feeZentra Arena pays the transfer

The match fee

The winner gets everything that was staked, less 10%. The fee comes out of the pot, not out of your wallet on top of your stake. If you lose, you lose your stake and nothing more.

In a one-on-one match, a winner gets back their own stake plus 80% of their opponent's. At ₦1,000 each, the winner ends ₦800 up and the loser ₦1,000 down.

Stake limits

  • Challenges and tournaments: ₦100 to ₦10,000 per player.
  • Match-ups: ₦2,000 to ₦10,000 per player, for the whole series.
  • Quick Match: ₦200 or ₦500.
  • Club league boards: a stake set for each division, currently ₦200.

Against a computer player

If you beat a computer player, you're paid the same as against a person: the stakes less 10%. If it beats you, your stake goes to Zentra Arena.

Other fees

WhatFee
Gifts10% of each gift. The player gets 90% straight away.
Starting a clubA one-off fee, currently ₦5,000. Joining a club is free.
Deposits by one-time accountPaystack's transfer fee is added to the amount you send, so the amount you chose reaches your wallet. The exact amount to send is shown before you pay.
Deposits to your permanent accountNo fee to you. What you send is credited in full.
WithdrawalsNo fee to you. Zentra Arena pays the bank transfer fee.

Refunds

You get your stake back in full, with no fee taken, when a match is cancelled before it's played. That covers a match nobody joins, a Quick Match pairing where you don't both get ready, and a club game neither player turns up for. See Clocks, resigning and rematches.

What Zentra Arena doesn't do

  • No bonuses, free bets or deposit offers.
  • No interest on your balance. Your wallet isn't a bank account.
18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
