Clubs and watching

Profiles, following and leaderboards

Your Zentra Arena profile, following players, starring games, Explore and the leaderboards.

On this page
  1. Your profile
  2. Following
  3. Starring games
  4. Explore
  5. Leaderboards

Zentra Arena is built around real people. Profiles show how you play, following lets you keep up with friends, and Explore helps you find matches, players and clubs.

Your profile

  • Name and photo: a display name, a username, a photo and a short bio. Your real name, used for payouts, isn't shown.
  • Ratings: your rating in each game you play. See Ratings.
  • Achievements: the badges you've earned. See Achievements.
  • Clubs: the clubs you're in.
  • Starred games: finished games you've starred as favourites, for anyone to look at.
  • Head to head: on someone else's profile, your record against them.
  • Online status: online, active in the last hour, or offline.

You can edit your profile from the Profile tab. Keep it respectful: no hate, threats, scams or links in names, photos or bios.

Screens from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

Following

  • Follow anyone from their profile. Your followers and the people you follow are listed on your profile.
  • People you follow who follow you back are your friends. By default, only friends can watch your games.
  • When friends are playing, they show in the friends playing now row, so you can watch.
  • Blocking someone unfollows you both ways, and neither of you can follow the other again. See Blocking and reporting.

Starring games

Star any finished game to keep it as a favourite on your profile. A game of yours starred by 10 people earns the Fan favourite badge. You can hide the stars count from your profile in the Menu.

Explore

TabWhat's there
For YouOpen matches you might like and players you may know.

ClubsClubs to join, and the league tables for each game.

A screen from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

Leaderboards

  • Rating: the top players in each game by rating.
  • ZAR (Zentra score): a top 100 overall and for each game. It's worked out from games against people only: how often you win, how many games you've won, the stakes, and how strong your opponents were. Games against computer players don't count towards it.

Your own place is shown at the bottom, even when you're not in the top 100.
