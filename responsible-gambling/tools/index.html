Safety and help

Limits, breaks and self-exclusion

Deposit limits, breaks of 24 hours to 30 days, self-exclusion for 6 months, 1 year or 5 years, and reality checks.

On this page
  1. Deposit limits
  2. Take a break
  3. Self-exclusion
  4. During a break or self-exclusion
  5. Reality check
  6. Blocking

Zentra Arena gives you tools to stay in control. You'll find them all in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Each one is described below, with exactly what it does and doesn't stop.

Deposit limits

  • Set the most you can deposit in any 24 hours, any 7 days or any 30 days, or all three. Each is a rolling window, not a calendar day or month.
  • A limit can be from ₦500 to ₦5,000,000.
  • Lowering a limit works at once.
  • Raising or removing a limit waits 24 hours. You can cancel the change during that time. The wait gives you a day to think it over.
  • The app shows how much you can still deposit before you reach your limit.
  • Money sent to your permanent deposit account above your limit is held, not lost. It's credited automatically once your limit allows.

A screen from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

Take a break

Lengths24h · 3d · 7d · 30dyou choose

A break is a pause from playing for real money.

Self-exclusion

Lengths6 months · 1 year · 5 yearsyou choose

Self-exclusion is for when you want to stop for a long time. It works like a break, for longer.

A screen from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

During a break or self-exclusion

StoppedStill works
Starting, joining or accepting any match, including Quick Match, rematches and club gamesMatches you'd already started finish and are settled
DepositsWithdrawals
Sending giftsRefunds of cancelled matches
Being picked for a league fixtureSigning in and seeing your account

Nobody can join a match you created before your break, so it won't start while you're away.

A break or self-exclusion can't be ended early from the app. Only Zentra Arena's support team can lift one, and only if you ask in writing. That's on purpose: it keeps the decision you made when you set it.

Reality check

  • A reminder while you play, after every 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or off. It's set to 1 hour to start with.
  • It shows how long you've been playing and how today is going: what you've staked and won.

A screen from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

Blocking

If someone pressures you to play, block them. They can't invite, challenge or gift you, follow you or watch your games. See Blocking and reporting.

Need more?

Our support team can close your account or help you exclude yourself. Write to zenn.games.official@gmail.com or use support in the app. Zentra Arena may also limit an account if we see signs that play is causing harm. Free, private help is listed on Play responsibly.
