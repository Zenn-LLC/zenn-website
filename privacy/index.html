Zentra Arena Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-14. This policy explains what Zentra Arena collects about you, why, who it's shared with, how long it's kept, and the choices and rights you have. It covers the Zentra Arena app at zentraarena.com and this website.

Who we are

Zentra Arena is run by Zenn Interactive Limited, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 9153066) and based in Lagos. We decide how your information is used. Contact us about anything in this policy at zenn.games.official@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign up and use your account

  • Your phone number, which you sign in with and which we text codes to.
  • Your real name, used for payouts and identity checks. It isn't shown on your profile.
  • Your username, display name, and, if you add them, a photo, a bio and your favourite games.
  • Your sign-in PIN and wallet PINs, which we store only in a scrambled form we can't read back.
  • That you confirmed you're 18 or older and accepted the terms, and when.
  • The devices you sign in from (a device identifier and model), and the internet address used for each sign-in, to keep your account safe.
  • Your settings: game settings, notification choices, who can watch your games, match chat setting, and your limits, breaks and reality-check choice.

Money

  • Your wallets and every transaction: deposits, stakes, winnings, refunds, withdrawals and gifts.
  • The bank account you withdraw to, and the permanent deposit account number Paystack gives you if you set one up.
  • If you verify your identity: the bank account you verified with, and the result. Your BVN is sent to Paystack for the check. We don't store it.

Playing

  • Every move in every game, the results, your ratings, reviews and achievements.
  • Who you follow, who follows you, the games you star, the players you block, and your clubs.
  • Match chat and club chat messages, and any reports you make or that are made about you.
  • Messages you send to support, and our replies.
  • If you joined through a promoter's link, which promoter.

On your device

The app keeps a few things in your browser's storage: what keeps you signed in, a promoter code until you sign up, and some display and sound preferences. If you allow notifications, your browser gives us an address to send them to. We don't use advertising trackers or analytics services in the app. This website uses no cookies.

Why we use it

PurposeWhy we're allowed to
Running your account, your matches and your moneyTo provide the service you signed up for
Paying winnings and withdrawals, and taking depositsTo provide the service, and because the law requires financial records
Checking your identity and ageThe law, and keeping under-18s and fraud out
Fair play: reviewing games, spotting cheating and money being passed between accountsOur legitimate interest in fair games, and the terms you accepted
Keeping accounts safe: sign-in codes, device checks, stopping abuse in chatOur legitimate interest in security, and yours
Your limits, breaks, self-exclusion and reality checksTo protect you, as you asked, and our duty to play our part in safer gambling
Answering support requests and complaintsTo provide the service
NotificationsYour choice. You can turn them off

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to show you advertising.

What other people can see

  • Your profile: username, display name, photo, bio, ratings, achievements, clubs, starred games and followers. Your real name and phone number are never shown.
  • Your games, to people you allow to watch, and to your opponents.
  • Gifts you send are shown by name to everyone in that game.
  • Your place on leaderboards.

Who we share it with

  • Paystack, our payment partner, for deposits, withdrawals, deposit accounts and identity checks.
  • An SMS provider, to text you codes.
  • Hosting and storage providers that run our servers, databases and stored photos for us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to prevent fraud or protect someone's safety.

These providers only use your information to do the work for us. Some of them may store or process it outside Nigeria. When that happens we rely on the safeguards Nigerian data protection law allows.

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them.

How long we keep it

  • Match chat and club chat: 30 days.
  • Transactions and other money records: as long as the law requires, even after an account is closed.
  • Closed accounts: we remove your personal details, and keep the money records the law requires.
  • Everything else: while your account is open, and after that only as long as we need it for the reasons above, such as a dispute or a fair-play case.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy,
  • correct anything that's wrong,
  • delete your information, where we don't have to keep it,
  • stop or limit some uses of it, or object to them,
  • give you your information in a form you can take elsewhere.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to zenn.games.official@gmail.com. We'll reply within the time the law allows. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Under 18s

Zentra Arena is only for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we close their accounts when we find them.

Keeping it safe

PINs are stored scrambled, connections to Zentra Arena are encrypted, and access to your information is limited to Zentra Arena's team. No system is perfectly secure. If something went wrong that put you at risk, we would tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its version date, and tell you in the app when the change matters.
