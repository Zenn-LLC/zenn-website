Safety and help

Support and disputes

How to raise a problem with a match or anything else, and how Zentra answers.

On this page
  1. Raising a problem
  2. How long it takes
  3. What helps us help you
  4. Disputes about results
  5. If you're not happy with our answer
  6. Other ways to reach us

If something goes wrong with a match, your money or your account, tell us from the app. Zentra's team answers there, and you'll get a notification when we do.

Raising a problem

  1. Tap the speech bubble on HomeIt opens Support.
  2. Choose what it's aboutA problem with a match: a result, a player, your stake or winnings. Or Something else.
  3. Pick the matchFor a match problem, choose it from your matches in the last 30 days. For an older one, choose "Something else" and tell us which.
  4. Tell us what happenedBe specific: what you expected, what happened, and when.
  5. Wait for our answerIt arrives in the same conversation, under "Your messages", with a notification.

How long it takes

We reply to complaints within 14 days.

What helps us help you

  • For a deposit: the amount, the time you sent it, and your bank.
  • For a withdrawal: the amount and date, and what your activity shows.
  • For a match: what went wrong, and at what point in the game.
  • For a player: what they did. If it was in chat, report the message from the chat as well.

Disputes about results

Results are final once a match is settled, unless Zentra voids it for cheating under the fair-play rules. If a fault on our side stopped a match, we refund it or settle it under the game's rules. Every game is recorded move by move, so we can check what happened.

If you're not happy with our answer

These terms are governed by Nigerian law. If we can't settle a dispute between us, the courts of Lagos State decide it. Nothing in our terms takes away rights you have under Nigerian law. See section 13 of the Terms of Service.

Other ways to reach us

Email zenn.games.official@gmail.com, for example if you can't sign in. We don't offer phone support. More on Contact.
