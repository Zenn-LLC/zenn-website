Play Zentra in your browser

Zentra runs in your phone or computer browser at zentra.com.ng. What you need before you sign up.

On this page
  1. What you need
  2. Phone or computer
  3. Check the address
  4. New here?

Zentra is a web app. Open it in your browser and play: there's nothing to download and nothing to update.

Open zentra.com.ng

What you need

  • To be 18 or older and living in Nigeria.
  • A Nigerian phone number that can receive texts. It becomes your sign-in.
  • A bank account in your own name, for adding and withdrawing money.
  • A recent browser on a phone or computer, and a steady internet connection. Matches use a live connection, and your clock keeps running if you drop out.

A screen from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

Phone or computer

Zentra is built for phones first, and every game works on a touch screen. On a computer you get a bigger board or table, and the same account works on both. Sign in with your phone number and 6-digit PIN wherever you are.

Check the address

The only address for Zentra is zentra.com.ng. Zentra will never ask for your PIN, a one-time code or your bank details in a message, a call or on another website. If you see a site that looks like Zentra at another address, don't sign in there. Tell us at zenn.games.official@gmail.com. More in Account security.

New here?

Read Getting started for the sign-up steps, or What is Zentra for the short version of how it all works.

18+

Zentra is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
