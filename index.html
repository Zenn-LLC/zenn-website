How the money works

  • Your stake is held from the moment you join until the match ends.
  • The winner gets the stakes less Zentra's 10% fee. If a match never starts, everyone gets their stake back.
  • Add money by bank transfer through Paystack. Withdraw to your own bank account up to 3 times a day.
  • Your money stays yours. You can always withdraw it, even during a break.
Stakes and fees

Skill decides it

  • Every player has a rating for each game that moves with every result.
  • Every game you play can be reviewed move by move, with your accuracy and notes.
  • Real-money games between people are reviewed for cheating.
  • Computer players are labelled "Computer" and are never set up to make you lose.
Fair play

Start in four steps

  1. Open zentra.com.ngSign up with your Nigerian phone number. You must be 18 or older.
  2. Set your PINsA 6-digit PIN signs you in. Your wallet gets its own 4-digit PIN, asked for whenever money leaves it.
  3. Add moneyPick an amount and send it by bank transfer. Your wallet is credited automatically.
  4. PlayJoin Quick Match, accept an invite or create your own match.
