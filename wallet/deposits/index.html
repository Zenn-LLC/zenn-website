Money

Adding money to Zentra

Add money by bank transfer through Paystack, or set up a permanent Zentra account number. Deposit limits and held deposits explained.

On this page
  1. One-time account
  2. Your permanent account number
  3. Deposit limits
  4. During a break or self-exclusion
  5. If a deposit doesn't arrive

You add money to Zentra by bank transfer. Payments are handled by Paystack, Zentra's payment partner. There are two ways to do it.

One-time account

  1. Tap DepositChoose the wallet and pick an amount, or tap ₦100, ₦200, ₦500, ₦1,000, ₦2,000 or ₦5,000.
  2. Get an account numberZentra shows a bank account number and the exact amount to send. Paystack's transfer fee is added on top, so the amount you chose is what reaches your wallet.
  3. Send it within 10 minutesTransfer exactly that amount from your banking app before the account expires.
  4. Your wallet is creditedAs soon as the money arrives, automatically. You'll get a notification.

Screens from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

Your permanent account number

Once you've verified your identity, you can activate a dedicated deposit account: a bank account number that's yours to keep. Transfer any amount to it at any time from your banking app, and your wallet is credited automatically. What you send is credited in full.

A screen from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

Deposit limits

You can set the most you're able to deposit in any 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, from Menu → Limits and breaks. Lowering a limit works at once. Raising or removing one waits 24 hours. See Limits, breaks and self-exclusion.

If you send money to your permanent account that would take you over your limit, the extra is held, not lost. It's credited automatically once your limit allows. You can see held money in the app.

During a break or self-exclusion

You can't start a new deposit. Money sent to your permanent account during a break is credited so you can withdraw it, but it can't be staked until the break ends.

If a deposit doesn't arrive

  • Check that you sent the exact amount shown, to the account shown, within 10 minutes.
  • Bank transfers are sometimes slow. Give it a little time before raising it.
  • If it still hasn't arrived, contact support from the app with the time, amount and your bank. See Support and disputes.

Only send money to account numbers shown inside the Zentra app. Zentra will never message you an account number to pay into.

18+

Zentra is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
