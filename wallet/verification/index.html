Verifying your identity on Zentra Arena

What identity verification unlocks on Zentra Arena, what you need for it and how long it takes.

On this page
  1. What it unlocks
  2. How to verify
  3. If it fails
  4. What happens to your details
  5. Age checks

You can sign up, deposit and play without verifying your identity. Verifying unlocks more, and it helps keep accounts in their real owners' hands. It's done in the app, using your BVN and a bank account in your name.

What it unlocks

Not verifiedVerified
Wallets1Up to 3
WithdrawalsTo the bank account that matches your phone numberTo any bank account in your name
Permanent deposit accountNoYes
Creating tournamentsNo (you can join them)Yes
Starting a clubNo (you can join one)Yes

How to verify

  1. Open verificationFrom the Menu, or when the app asks for it, for example when you start a club or set up a permanent deposit account.
  2. Enter your BVNYour 11-digit Bank Verification Number.
  3. Confirm a bank account linked to itEnter the account number and choose the bank. It must be an account already registered to your BVN.
  4. Wait for the checkPaystack checks your BVN, the account and the name on your Zentra Arena account against each other. The app tells you when it's done.

Screens from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

If it fails

The app tells you why. The most common reason is a name that doesn't match: your Zentra Arena account must be in the same name as your bank account and BVN. Fix the details and try again. If you're stuck, contact support.

What happens to your details

  • Your BVN is passed to Paystack for the check. Zentra Arena doesn't store it.
  • Zentra Arena keeps the bank account details you verified with, to pay withdrawals to it.
  • More in the Privacy Policy.

Age checks

Zentra Arena is for people 18 and over. We may ask you to prove your age at any time. Accounts of anyone under 18 are closed and their balance returned.

18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
