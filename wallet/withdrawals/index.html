Money

Withdrawing from Zentra Arena

Withdraw to your own bank account up to 3 times a day: ₦100 to ₦30,000 each time and ₦50,000 a day.

On this page
  1. How to withdraw
  2. Whose account
  3. Withdrawals always work
  4. If something goes wrong

Withdraw your balance to a bank account in your own name, from the Wallet tab. Zentra Arena pays the transfer fee.

Each time₦100–₦30,000per withdrawal
Each day₦50,000in total
How often3 a daycounted by the Lagos day
FeeNoneto you

Zentra Arena can change these numbers. The app always shows how many withdrawals you have left today.

How to withdraw

  1. Tap WithdrawChoose the wallet and the amount.
  2. Choose the bank accountPick your bank and enter your account number. Zentra Arena checks the account name with the bank before sending.
  3. Enter your wallet PINThe 4-digit PIN for that wallet. Tap to confirm.
  4. Wait for the transferIt shows as pending in your activity, then done. If a transfer fails, the money goes back to your wallet.

Screens from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

Whose account

  • Withdrawals only go to a bank account in your name.
  • Until you've verified your identity, it has to be the account that matches your phone number.

Withdrawals always work

You can withdraw during a break or self-exclusion. Your money stays yours. Zentra Arena may hold a withdrawal while it checks it for fraud or a mistake, and will tell you if it does.

If something goes wrong

If a withdrawal shows as failed, check your balance: the money should be back in your wallet. If it says done but hasn't reached your bank, give your bank a little time, then contact support from the app with the date and amount. See Support and disputes.

18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
