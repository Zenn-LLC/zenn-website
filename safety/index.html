Safety and help

Blocking and reporting on Zentra

What blocking someone does on Zentra, and how to report a chat message or a player.

On this page
  1. Blocking someone
  2. Reporting
  3. How everyone should behave
  4. Scams to watch for
  5. Pressure to play

You decide who you deal with on Zentra. Block anyone you don't want contact with, and report anything that breaks the rules.

Blocking someone

Block a player from their profile. You can see and undo your blocks in the Menu under blocked players.

Blocking works both ways. Once you block someone:

  • Neither of you can invite or challenge the other, or send the other a gift.
  • You stop following each other, and neither can follow the other again.
  • Neither can watch the other's games.
  • You don't see each other in search or in club chat.
  • They can't join your open matches or ask you for a rematch.
  • If you're both picked for the same board in a league fixture, that board is void.

Quick Match still pairs whoever is waiting, so you could meet in the queue. You can turn off match chat in the Menu if you want no messages from anyone.

A screen from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

Reporting

  • Match chat: report a message from the chat. Zentra's team can delete it and stop the sender from using match chat.
  • Club chat: tap a message and choose "Report to Zentra". Club owners and admins can also delete messages and mute members.
  • A player or a match: use Support in the app. See Support and disputes.

How everyone should behave

Be respectful in names, photos, bios and chats. No hate, threats, scams or links. Zentra may remove content, mute players or close accounts that break these rules. This is section 10 of the Terms of Service.

Scams to watch for

  • Anyone asking you to pay them outside the app to "arrange" a result, or offering to sell an account. Both are against the rules.
  • Anyone asking for your PIN, a code sent to your phone, or your bank details. Zentra never asks for these.
  • Links to sites that look like Zentra. The only address is zentra.com.ng.

More in Account security.

Pressure to play

If someone keeps pushing you to play or stake more, block them. The limits, breaks and self-exclusion tools are there if you want help sticking to your own limits.
