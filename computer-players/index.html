Computer players on Zentra Arena

When you might play a computer player on Zentra Arena, how strong it is, how it is labelled and what happens to your stake.

On this page
  1. When you'll meet one
  2. How you can tell
  3. How strong it is
  4. The money
  5. Ratings and achievements
  6. What computer players are for

Zentra Arena has computer players so that you can get a game when nobody else is waiting. You always know when you're playing one, and they are never set up to make you lose.

When you'll meet one

  • Only in Quick Match, one-on-one, when no person is free at your game and stake.
  • Never in matches you create, tournaments, match-ups, club games or league fixtures.
  • A computer player can accept a rematch after a Quick Match, like a person can.

How you can tell

A computer player has a name and a face, and its profile says Computer next to the name, with the games it plays. It can't be followed. Computer players never chat, and they can't be sent gifts.

A screen from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

How strong it is

  • It has no fixed rating of its own. Its strength is chosen at random for each match, within 25 points of your current rating in that game, either side.
  • If your rating was recently higher, or the stake is bigger, the lower end of that range is lifted. It's never more than 25 points above your rating.
  • Its strength never changes during a match. It doesn't play harder when you're winning or softer when you're losing.
  • Each strength level is rated from real results and corrected over time, so "within 25 points" stays true.

Zentra Arena doesn't decide in advance who wins a game against a computer player, and doesn't aim for any win rate. Against a player at your level, you should expect to win about half the time.

The money

Stakes against a computer player are real. Zentra Arena is on the other side of the match:

  • If you win, you're paid exactly as you would be against a person: the stakes less Zentra Arena's 10% fee.
  • If the computer player wins, your stake goes to Zentra Arena.

Ratings and achievements

Games against computer players move your rating like any other game, and they count towards achievements.

What computer players are for

They mean a short wait in the queue rather than a long one, at any hour. If you'd rather play people only, create a match and invite someone, or play in your club, where computer players never appear.

18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
