Zentra Arena promoters and referral links

Promoters share a Zentra Arena link and earn a share of Zentra Arena's fee on games played by the people they bring. How it works.

On this page
  1. How the link works
  2. What a promoter earns
  3. Getting paid
  4. If a partnership changes
  5. Promoting Zentra Arena responsibly
  6. Become a promoter

Promoters are creators, communities and partners who bring new players to Zentra Arena. Each promoter gets a link. When someone signs up through it, the promoter earns a share of Zentra Arena's fee on that player's games.

How the link works

  • A promoter's link looks like zentraarena.com/r/YOURCODE.
  • When someone opens it, the code is remembered on their device until they sign up, even if they sign up on a later visit.
  • Signing up is what links the player to the promoter. Each player can be linked to one promoter only, at sign-up.
  • If a code has expired or been paused, the person can still sign up. They just won't be linked to anyone.

What a promoter earns

A promoter earns an agreed share of Zentra Arena's fee on the games their players play. It's never a share of stakes, deposits or winnings.

  • Games between people: the promoter earns on their player's part of the fee, whoever wins.
  • Games against a computer player: the promoter earns only when Zentra Arena came out ahead, that is, when their player got back less than they staked.
  • If a game is later voided, unpaid commission from it is reversed.

Because commission comes from Zentra Arena's fee, in games between people a promoter earns the same whether their player wins or loses.

Getting paid

  • Commission builds up as players play.
  • Zentra Arena pays promoters by bank transfer through Paystack, to a bank account that Zentra Arena's team has checked and confirmed.
  • A payout is never more than what's owed.

If a partnership changes

  • Paused: the link stops linking new players, but the promoter keeps earning on players already linked.
  • Ended: no new players and no further commission.

Promoting Zentra Arena responsibly

Zentra Arena is for adults aged 18 and over in Nigeria, and it doesn't offer bonuses or free bets. Promotion should say so honestly: no promises of easy money, and nothing aimed at under-18s. Point people to Play responsibly.

Become a promoter

Email zenn.games.official@gmail.com with who you are, where your audience is, and how you'd like to promote Zentra Arena. We'll get back to you. The brand page has the logo and colours.
