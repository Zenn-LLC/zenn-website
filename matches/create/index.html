Matches

Create or join a match on Zentra Arena

Set up a challenge, tournament or match-up: pick the game, the stake and who can join. Or join one with a match code.

On this page
  1. Create a match
  2. When it starts
  3. Join a match
  4. If nobody joins

Create Match sets up a match exactly how you want it: the game, the stake and who plays. It takes under a minute. To join someone else's match, you only need its code.

Create a match

Tap Create Match on Home. A few short steps follow, and you can go back at any point.

  1. Choose the kind of matchChallenge: one on one. Tournament: a bracket of 4 to 256 players. Match-up: a best-of series against one person.
  2. Choose the gameChess, Whot, Lie, Ludo or Pool.
  3. Set the stake and walletWhat each player puts in: ₦100 to ₦10,000 (at least ₦2,000 for a match-up). Type an amount or tap ₦500, ₦1,000, ₦2,000 or ₦5,000, then pick which wallet pays.
  4. Tournament size or series lengthFor a tournament, pick 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 or 256 players. Each size shows its pot. For a match-up, pick best of 3, 5, 7, 9 or 11.
  5. Who can joinOpen: anyone can find and join it. Invite only: you pick exactly who, by username. A match-up is always invite only.
  6. Name and descriptionOptional. Add a title or house notes for the people joining.
  7. Review and createCheck the summary, including what the winner takes, then create it. Your stake is held straight away.

When it's made, you get a match code to copy and share.

To create a tournament, you need to have verified your identity. Anyone can join one.

Screens from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

When it starts

  • Challenges and tournaments start the moment the last player joins, with 24 hours on each player's clock. Nobody needs to be online at the same time.
  • Match-ups are played live: both players get ready before each game, with normal clocks. See Match-ups.

Join a match

  • With a code: tap Load Match on Home and enter the code you were sent.
  • From an invite: open it from Notifications, check the stake and accept.
  • Open matches: open matches you might like show up on Explore.

Joining takes your stake from the wallet you choose, the same amount everyone else staked.

A screen from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

If nobody joins

  • If someone you invited declines, or once the match is 3 hours old, you can open it to everyone, or cancel it and get your stake back.
  • A match still not full after 6 hours is cancelled automatically, and every stake is returned.
18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
