Quick Match on Zentra Arena

Join the queue at ₦200 or ₦500 and get paired with another player, or join a tournament of four.

Quick Match is the fastest way to play. Pick a game and a stake, join the queue, and you're paired with the next player waiting at the same stake. No invite needed.

One on one₦200 · ₦500stake each
Tournament of 4₦200 · ₦500stake each
Clock15 mineach (Ludo 20)

How it works

  1. Tap Quick Match on HomeChoose one on one or a tournament of four.
  2. Pick a game and a stakeEach stake shows how many people are waiting. You need the stake in your wallet.
  3. Wait to be pairedPlayers are paired in the order they joined. If your connection drops, you keep your place for 20 seconds.
  4. Get readyWhen you're paired, both players tap Ready. The game starts as soon as you both have. If you don't both get ready within a minute, both stakes are returned.

Tournaments of four

A four-player queue waits until four people have joined at the same stake. The bracket has two semi-finals and a final. Each game is one on one, and the winner of the final takes the pot. Tournament queues are only ever filled by people, never by computer players. More on Tournaments.

Computer players

In the one-on-one queue, if no person is free, you may be paired with a computer player. Its profile says Computer. It plays within 25 points of your rating, and it is never set up to make you lose. If it wins, your stake goes to Zentra Arena. If you win, you're paid as normal.

After the game

Either player can ask for a rematch at the same stake. Your rating changes with the result, and the game's review is ready shortly after.

When a queue is paused

Zentra Arena sometimes pauses a game's queue, for example during maintenance. A paused queue shows a message and can't be joined until it's back.

18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
