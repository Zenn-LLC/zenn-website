Clocks, resigning and rematches

What happens when your clock runs out, when you resign, when a match never starts, and how to ask for a rematch.

On this page
  1. Clocks
  2. Resigning
  3. Losing your connection
  4. Matches that never start
  5. Rematches
  6. If something goes wrong on our side

Every game on Zentra Arena has a clock for each player, and every game ends with a winner. Here's what happens at the edges: running out of time, giving up, dropping out, and playing again.

Clocks

MatchClock per player
Quick Match, rematches, match-ups, club games15 minutes (Ludo 20 minutes) for the whole game
Challenges and tournaments you create24 hours for the whole game
  • Your clock only runs on your turn.
  • If your clock reaches zero, you lose the game.
  • In chess, a drawn position goes to the player with more time left.

Resigning

Every game has a resign button. Resigning ends the game straight away and your opponent wins. It's settled like any other ending: the winner is paid and ratings change.

Screens from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

Losing your connection

If you drop out during a game, your clock keeps running on your turn. Come back as soon as you can: you rejoin the same game where you left it. If your clock runs out first, you lose. In the Quick Match queue, you keep your place for 20 seconds if your connection drops.

Matches that never start

  • Quick Match: if both players don't get ready within a minute of being paired, both stakes are returned.
  • Matches you create: if it isn't full after 6 hours, it's cancelled and refunded. You can cancel sooner once it's 3 hours old, or once everyone invited has declined.
  • Match-ups: if neither player starts the first game within 30 minutes, both stakes are returned.
  • Club games: if you don't show up within 5 minutes of the start time and your opponent does, you lose. If neither shows, both are refunded.

Rematches

  • After a one-on-one game, either player can ask for a rematch at the same stake.
  • The other player has 30 seconds to accept. If you both ask at once, it counts as an accept.
  • Once accepted, a new match is created, both stakes are taken, and the game starts at once with the normal clock.
  • You can't ask someone who has blocked you, or whom you've blocked, for a rematch.

A screen from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

If something goes wrong on our side

If a fault at Zentra Arena stops a match, we refund it or settle it under the game's rules. If you think a result is wrong, raise it from the app. See Support and disputes.

18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
