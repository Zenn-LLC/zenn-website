What is Zentra Arena?

Zentra Arena is a Nigerian platform for playing skill games against real people, with a stake on each match. What it is, who runs it and how it makes money.

On this page
  1. What you can do on Zentra Arena
  2. How Zentra Arena makes money
  3. Skill, and a little luck
  4. Who runs it
  5. Who it's for

Zentra Arena is a place to play games you already know against other people, with money on the match. You put in a stake, the better player on the day wins, and the winner is paid the stakes less Zentra Arena's fee. It runs in your web browser at zentra.com.ng, on a phone or a computer.

What you can do on Zentra Arena

  • Play five games one on one: Chess, Whot, Lie, Ludo and Pool.
  • Choose how you play: Quick Match pairs you with whoever is waiting at your stake. You can also create a challenge, run a tournament or set up a best-of match-up.
  • Join a club for your game, chat with members, play them at set times, and compete against other clubs in leagues.
  • Watch other people's games live, react, and send a gift to a player you're watching.
  • Get better: each game has its own rating, every game you play can be reviewed move by move, and there are achievements to collect.

How Zentra Arena makes money

Zentra Arena keeps 10% of what is staked in each match. In a ₦1,000 challenge, both players stake ₦1,000, so ₦2,000 is staked. The winner gets ₦1,800 and Zentra Arena keeps ₦200. Zentra Arena also keeps 10% of each gift, and charges a fee to start a club. That's all of it. See Stakes and fees for every case.

Zentra Arena also has computer players. You can meet one in Quick Match when no person is free. When a computer player wins, the stake goes to Zentra Arena. When you win, you are paid as normal. A computer player plays at about your own rating and is never set up to make you lose.

Skill, and a little luck

Chess and Pool are decided by how you play. Whot, Lie and Ludo have shuffled cards and dice, so one game can go either way. Over many games, the better player wins more often, and that is what ratings measure. Treat any single match as a game, not a sure thing.

Who runs it

Zentra Arena is built and run by Zenn Interactive Limited, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 9153066) and based in Lagos. Payments go through Paystack. You can reach us at zenn.games.official@gmail.com.

Who it's for

Zentra Arena is for adults in Nigeria. You must be 18 or older to sign up, and Zentra Arena closes any account belonging to someone under 18. It is meant to be fun. If it stops being fun, the app has limits, breaks and self-exclusion, and there is free help.
