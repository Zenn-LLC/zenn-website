Skill and fairness

Achievements on Zentra

Badges for first wins, streaks, rating milestones, great moves and rare moments. None of them rewards spending or playing more.

On this page
  1. The rules
  2. Getting started
  3. Streaks
  4. Mastery
  5. Reviews
  6. Social
  7. Rare
  8. Secret

Achievements are badges for moments worth remembering: first wins, streaks, rating milestones, great moves and rare finishes. They show on your profile. None of them is for spending money or playing more hours, and none comes with a reward. They're just for you.

The rules

  • Nothing rewards stakes, deposits or the number of games you play in a day.
  • Games against computer players count.
  • Games you played before achievements arrived count too, where Zentra has the record.
  • Once earned, a badge is yours to keep.

Streaks

BadgeHow to earn it
On fireWin 3, 5 or 10 games in a row.
RegularPlay on 7, 30, 100, 200, 300 or 365 days in a row (by the Lagos day).

Mastery

BadgeHow to earn it
VeteranPlay 100 games of a game.
All-rounderWin a game of every kind.
Rating milestonesReach set ratings in each game (below).
GameRating milestones
Chess1,200 · 1,500 · 1,800 · 2,100
Pool1,200 · 1,400 · 1,600 · 1,800
Lie1,150 · 1,300 · 1,450
Ludo1,100 · 1,200 · 1,300
Whot1,050 · 1,125 · 1,200

The milestones differ because ratings spread out more in some games than others. Zentra may adjust them. If a milestone is lowered, everyone who now qualifies gets it, and nobody loses one.

Social

BadgeHow to earn it
Fan favouriteHave one of your games starred by 10 people.
Crowd pullerHave 10 people watching your game at once.
GenerousSend a gift to a player you're watching.

Rare

BadgeHow to earn it
Giant killerBeat someone rated 300 or more above you.
Lightning mateChess: checkmate in under 10 moves.
Clean sweepPool: win before your opponent pots one of theirs.
You're the STARWhot: win on a Whot 20, calling for stars.
Last of the marketWhot: take the market's last card and still win.
Honest to a faultLie: get checked 5 times in a game, telling the truth every time.

Secret

There are seven secret badges. You'll find out what they are when you earn one, or when you see one on someone's profile.
