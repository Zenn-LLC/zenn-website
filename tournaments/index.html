Tournaments on Zentra Arena

Tournaments of 4 to 256 players on Zentra Arena: brackets, stakes, the pot and the winner's prize.

On this page
  1. Two ways in
  2. How the bracket works
  3. The pot
  4. If it doesn't fill
  5. Club tournaments
  6. Achievements

A tournament is a knockout bracket. Everyone stakes the same amount, every game is one on one, and the player who wins the final takes the pot.

Two ways in

Quick tournaments of 4

From Quick Match, at ₦200 or ₦500 a player. The bracket fills with the next four people to join, and games use the normal 15-minute clock (20 for Ludo). Only people play in these, never computer players.

Tournaments you create

From Create Match: 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 or 256 players, at any stake from ₦100 to ₦10,000. Open or invite only. Games use 24-hour clocks. Creating one needs a verified identity.

How the bracket works

  • Players are paired for the first round. Each pair plays one game.
  • Winners go through to the next round. Losers are out.
  • The rounds go on until two players are left for the final.
  • In a tournament you created, each new round starts as soon as it's set, with fresh 24-hour clocks.

The pot

The pot is every player's stake added together, less Zentra Arena's 10% fee. It all goes to the winner of the final.

ExampleTotal stakedWinner gets
8 players at ₦1,000₦8,000₦7,200
16 players at ₦500₦8,000₦7,200
32 players at ₦2,000₦64,000₦57,600

The pot for your bracket size is shown live while you set the tournament up, so you see it before you create it.

If it doesn't fill

A tournament that isn't full after 6 hours is cancelled, and every stake is returned. The creator can cancel sooner once it's 3 hours old, or once everyone invited has declined.

Club tournaments

Clubs run their own tournaments of 4 or 8 players with a fixed start time. If one isn't full at the start time, it's cancelled and refunded. See Club chat and games.

Achievements

Two secret achievements are for tournaments: winning one of 8 or more players, and winning one as its lowest-rated player.

18+

Zentra Arena is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
