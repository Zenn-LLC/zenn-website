Clubs and watching

Club leagues on Zentra

Clubs play each other in divisions: seasons, fixtures of five boards, points, promotion and trophies.

On this page

  7. Trophies

In a league, clubs play other clubs for the same game. Each division plays a season of fixtures twice a week, and the table decides who goes up and who goes down.

Entering

  • The club's owner enters the club. It needs at least 5 members.
  • Once entered, the club stays in each new season until the owner takes it out.
  • Zentra starts each season for each game.

Divisions and seasons

  • Clubs are split into divisions of about ten. Clubs back from last season start where they finished, and new clubs start below, by strength.
  • Every club in a division plays every other club once.
  • Fixtures kick off on Wednesdays and Saturdays at 8 pm, Lagos time.
  • When every fixture is played, the top two go up and the bottom two go down.

A screen from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

A fixture

  1. Say you can playMembers say they're available for the fixture and choose the wallet a stake would come from. Saying yes is agreeing to be staked if you're picked.
  2. The lineup is pickedThe owner or admins pick up to five from those available, until an hour before kick-off. Any places still empty are filled by the best-rated members who said yes.
  3. Boards are pairedAn hour before kick-off, both lineups are locked and paired by rating: board 1 is each side's best player, and so on.
  4. Kick-offEvery board starts at kick-off as a normal staked match at the division's stake, with normal clocks.

Scoring

ResultPoints
Win the fixture (more boards won)3
Level on boards1 each
Lose the fixture0

Tables are ordered by points, then board difference, then boards won. League tables for every game are on Explore.

When players don't turn up

  • The club games' 5-minute no-show rule applies on each board: a player who doesn't show loses that board.
  • If neither player on a board shows, the board is void and both stakes are returned.
  • A board with nobody on one side goes to the other side, and no money is staked on it.
  • A club that can field fewer than three players loses the fixture 0–5, and no games are made.
  • A board between two players who have blocked each other is void.

The money

Each board is an ordinary staked match: both players stake the division's stake, and the winner gets both stakes less Zentra's 10% fee. The stake is set for each division and is currently ₦200 everywhere. There's no prize pot for winning the league.

Nobody on a break, self-excluded or without the stake in the chosen wallet can be picked.

Trophies

When a season ends, clubs earn honours on their club page: champions, division winners and promoted.

18+

Zentra is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
