Clubs and watching

Clubs on Zentra Arena

Start or join a club for your game: members, roles, invite links and requests.

On this page

  2. Starting a club
  3. Roles

A club is a group of players for one game. Clubs have their own chat, play each other at set times, and compete against other clubs in leagues. Find them on Explore → Clubs.

Game1each club plays one game
Your clubs1 per gameup to five in all
MembersUp to 30per club

Joining a club

  • By invite link: open a link a member shared and you're in straight away.
  • By request: ask to join from the club's page. The owner or an admin accepts or declines.
  • You can be in one club for each game, so one Chess club, one Whot club and so on.
  • Joining is free.

Starting a club

  • You need a verified identity.
  • Starting one costs a one-off fee, currently ₦5,000, paid from a wallet with your wallet PIN.
  • Choose the game and a name, and upload a badge.
  • You become the club's owner.

Screens from the Zentra Arena app, with sample players and amounts.

Roles

RoleCan
OwnerEverything an admin can, plus make members admins (or back), remove admins, hand the club on to someone else, and enter the club in leagues.
AdminsAccept or decline join requests, share or renew the invite link, edit the description and badge, remove members, set up club tournaments, pick league lineups, and delete chat messages or mute members in chat.
MembersChat, challenge clubmates, join club tournaments and say they're available for league fixtures.

What clubs do

Chat and club games

A members-only chat, challenges between clubmates and club tournaments, all with set start times.

Read more

Leagues

Clubs play each other in divisions, five boards a fixture, twice a week. Finish in the top two to go up.

Read more

Good to know

  • Computer players are never in clubs.
  • Your clubs show on your profile, and a club's trophies show on its page.
  • If you've blocked someone, you won't see each other in club chat.
