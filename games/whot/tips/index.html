Whot tips for Zentra

How to win more Whot games: keeping counters, timing your special cards, counting what has been played and calling the right shape.

On this page
  1. Keep your answers
  2. Use your extra turns
  3. Save your Whot
  4. Count the cards
  5. Going to market on purpose
  6. Finishing

Whot has luck in the deal, but the same hands are won and lost by how the special cards are used. These are the habits that make the difference over many games.

Keep your answers

  • Hold a 2 or a 5 back when you can. A 2 is the only answer to Pick Two and a 5 the only answer to Pick Three. Holding one means a pick-up aimed at you can be sent back.
  • Pick-ups stack. If you play a 2 and your opponent answers with a 2, you now owe 4 unless you have another. Before starting a pick-up war, think about how many of that number are still out.

Use your extra turns

  • In a one-on-one game, Hold On (1), Suspension (8) and General Market (14) all give you another turn. Chain them to get rid of several cards in a row.
  • The best time for an extra-turn card is when it lets you change to a shape you hold a lot of.
  • General Market also makes your opponent draw. Late in the game, when they're down to one or two cards, that card matters most.

Save your Whot

  • A Whot 20 can be played on almost anything, so it's your way out when nothing else fits. Playing it early just to change shape often wastes it.
  • When you call a shape, call the one you hold most of, or one you know your opponent can't follow. Watch which shapes they go to market on.
  • A Whot doesn't answer a Pick Two or Pick Three, so it won't save you from a pick-up.

A screen from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

Count the cards

  • The deck is fixed: 54 cards, and you can see every card played. Stars only go up to 8, and crosses and squares have no 4 or 8.
  • If most of a shape has been played, your opponent is unlikely to have it. That's a good shape to change to.
  • Notice when your opponent goes to market. It usually means they had nothing to match the top card.

Going to market on purpose

Sometimes drawing a card is better than playing one, like keeping a 2 for defence or holding a shape you'll need later. A bigger hand isn't always worse if it gives you answers.

Finishing

  • Plan your last two or three cards: an extra-turn card followed by a matching card can finish the game in one turn.
  • If your opponent is down to one card, make them draw with a pick-up or General Market, or call a shape they've shown they don't have.
