Game settings on Zentra

The settings in each Zentra game: boards, pieces, card colours, cues, sounds and play helpers. None of them gives anyone an edge.

On this page
  1. Chess
  2. Whot
  3. Lie
  4. Ludo
  5. Pool
  6. In every game
  7. Chat and watching

Each game has its own settings, from the gear button in the game or from the Menu. Changes save to your account straight away. None of them changes what your opponent sees, and none gives anyone an edge.

Chess

SettingWhat it does
Pre-movesQueue moves during your opponent's turn.
Auto-queenPromote to a queen without asking.
Show legal movesDots where the piece can go.
BoardSixteen board colours.
PiecesPiece styles, including Gold, Ice, Ruby and Glow.
CoordinatesFiles and ranks on the board.

Whot

SettingWhat it does
Play the only cardWhen just one card can go, play it for you (never a Whot card).
Hand orderAs dealt, by shape or by number.
Card styleOutline or filled shapes.
Card coloursColour sets for the five shapes.

Lie

SettingWhat it does
Confirm before checkingAsk before calling a lie.
Hand order, card style, card coloursAs in Whot.

Ludo

SettingWhat it does
Move the only tokenWhen every way of playing the roll ends the same, make the move for you.
ColoursColour sets for the four sides.

Screens from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

Pool

SettingWhat it does
Aim sensitivityHow far the cue turns as you drag.
Cue, Balls, ClothThe look of your cue, the ball set and the table cloth.

A screen from the Zentra app, with sample players and amounts.

In every game

  • Sound: sound effects in that game.
  • Vibration: a buzz on your turn and at big moments, on phones that support it.

Chat and watching

Two more settings live in the Menu rather than in each game: who can watch your games, and whether match chat is on, limited to quick phrases, or off.
