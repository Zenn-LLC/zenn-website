Version 2026-11-13. This is the same text the Zentra app shows and asks you to accept when you sign up.

1. Who we are

Zentra is run by Zenn Interactive Limited, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 9153066) and based in Lagos. "We" and "us" means that company. You can reach us at zenn.games.official@gmail.com.

2. Who can play

  • You must be 18 or older. We may ask you to prove your age at any time, and we close accounts of anyone under 18 and return their balance.
  • One account per person, in your own name, with your own phone number. Accounts can't be shared, sold or handed over.
  • Some things, like more than one wallet, tournaments and a permanent deposit account, need you to verify your identity first.
  • Zentra is for people in Nigeria.

3. Your account and PIN

Keep your PIN to yourself. Everything done with your account is treated as done by you. If you think someone else has used it, tell us straight away. We may pause an account while we check it is safe.

4. Your money

  • Deposits are made by bank transfer through our payment partner, Paystack. Any fee is shown before you pay.
  • Withdrawals go to a bank account in your name. Until your identity is verified, it must be the account that matches your phone number. You can withdraw up to 3 times a day, at least ₦100 and at most ₦30,000 each time, and ₦50,000 a day. We can change these numbers, and the app always shows how many withdrawals you have left today.
  • We may hold a deposit or withdrawal while we check it for fraud or a mistake, and we'll tell you if we do.
  • Money in your wallet is yours. It earns no interest, and it isn't a bank account.

5. Matches and stakes

  • When you start or join a match, your stake is taken from your wallet and held until the match ends.
  • The winner is paid the stakes less Zentra's fee, which is 10% of what was staked. Each match shows what the winner takes before you join.
  • A match that never starts, or that is cancelled, is refunded. What happens when someone leaves, times out or loses connection is set by each game's rules in the app.
  • A match-up is a series of games for one stake: whoever wins most of the games wins the series.
  • Results are final once a match is settled, unless we void it under section 8.

6. Computer players

Zentra has computer players. Their profiles say "Computer". In the quick queue you may be matched with one when no person is free. A computer player plays at about your own rating. It is never set up to make you lose. Stakes against a computer player are real: if it wins, the stake goes to Zentra, and if you win, you are paid as normal.

7. Gifts

People watching a live match can send a player a gift of a set amount. The player gets 90% straight away and Zentra keeps 10%. Gifts can't be taken back. You can't gift a player in your own match, and you can't gift a computer player.

8. Fair play

You must not use chess engines or other help while you play, team up with another player, play from more than one account, or use a fault in the app to your advantage. We review games for this, and our team can watch any match live without being shown as a watcher. If we find it, we may void results, keep or return stakes, and close the accounts involved.

9. Playing responsibly

You can set deposit limits, take a break of 24 hours to 30 days, or exclude yourself for 6 months, 1 year or 5 years, from Menu → Limits and breaks. A break or exclusion can't be ended early from the app; only our support team can lift one, and only if you ask in writing. While one runs, you can't start or join matches, deposit or send gifts, but matches already started finish and you can still withdraw. We may also limit an account if we see signs that play is causing harm. See "Play responsibly" in the app for help.

10. How you behave

Be respectful in names, photos, bios and club chats. No hate, threats, scams or links. We may remove content, mute players in clubs, or close accounts that break these rules. You can block anyone. A blocked player can't invite, challenge or gift you, follow you or watch your games, and you won't see each other in search or club chat.

11. Closing your account

You can ask us to close your account at any time. We pay any balance to your bank account first, after the checks in section 4. We keep records of transactions for as long as the law requires.

12. Our responsibility

We work to keep Zentra running and fair, but we can't promise it will never be unavailable. If a fault on our side stops a match, we refund or settle it under the game's rules. We aren't responsible for stakes you chose to place, or for losses caused by your device or connection. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under Nigerian law.

13. Complaints and disputes

Tell us first at zenn.games.official@gmail.com, and we'll reply within 14 days. These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. If we can't settle a dispute between us, the courts of Lagos State decide it.

14. Changes to these terms

We'll tell you before these terms change. If the change matters, the app asks you to accept the new terms before you play again.
