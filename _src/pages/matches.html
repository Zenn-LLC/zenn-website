Every match on Zentra works the same way underneath. Each player stakes the same amount, Zentra holds the stakes while you play, and the winner is paid the stakes less Zentra's 10% fee. What changes is how you find an opponent and how long you have to play.

What happens to your stake

  1. You join, your stake is heldThe stake is taken from the wallet you choose and held by Zentra. It isn't in your balance while the match is on.
  2. The match is playedResults are decided on Zentra's servers, move by move.
  3. The winner is paidThe winner gets everything staked, less 10%. In a tournament, the player who wins the final gets it. The match shows what the winner takes before anyone joins.

Worked examples for every kind of match are on Stakes and fees.

When you get your stake back

  • A match nobody joins within 6 hours is cancelled, and every stake is returned.
  • If you created a match, you can cancel it and get your stake back once everyone you invited has declined, or once it's 3 hours old.
  • In Quick Match, if the two players don't both get ready within a minute of being paired, both stakes are returned.
  • If a fault on Zentra's side stops a match, it is refunded or settled under the game's rules.

More detail is on Clocks, resigning and rematches.

Open or by invite

A match you create can be open, so anyone can find it and join, or invite only, so only the people you pick can join. If someone you invited declines, or after 3 hours, you can open an invite-only match to everyone. Match-ups are always by invite.

Where to find your matches

The Home tab lists your matches. Invites and results arrive in Notifications. You can share any match with its match code, and people can join it with Load Match.

What you can't do

  • You can't start or join a match during a break or self-exclusion. Matches already started still finish.
  • You can't invite or challenge someone who has blocked you, or whom you've blocked.
