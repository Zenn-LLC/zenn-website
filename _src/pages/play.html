Zentra is a web app. Open it in your browser and play: there's nothing to download and nothing to update.

Open zentra.com.ng

What you need

  • To be 18 or older and living in Nigeria.
  • A Nigerian phone number that can receive texts. It becomes your sign-in.
  • A bank account in your own name, for adding and withdrawing money.
  • A recent browser on a phone or computer, and a steady internet connection. Matches use a live connection, and your clock keeps running if you drop out.

Phone or computer

Zentra is built for phones first, and every game works on a touch screen. On a computer you get a bigger board or table, and the same account works on both. Sign in with your phone number and 6-digit PIN wherever you are.

Check the address

The only address for Zentra is zentra.com.ng. Zentra will never ask for your PIN, a one-time code or your bank details in a message, a call or on another website. If you see a site that looks like Zentra at another address, don't sign in there. Tell us at {{EMAIL}}. More in Account security.
