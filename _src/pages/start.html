Signing up takes a few minutes. You need a Nigerian phone number that can receive texts, and you must be 18 or older.

Create your account

  1. Open ZentraGo to zentra.com.ng in your phone or computer browser and choose to create an account.
  2. Confirm your phone numberEnter your number and type in the 6-digit code we text you. This is the number you sign in with.
  3. Give your real nameYour name doesn't show on your profile. It is used for payouts and identity checks, so it must match your bank account.
  4. Confirm your age and accept the termsTick to say you're 18 or older and accept the Terms of Service. You can read Play responsibly from the same screen.
  5. Choose a 6-digit PINYou sign in with your phone number and this PIN. Keep it to yourself.
  6. Pick your games and a photoBoth are optional and can be changed later. Zentra uses your games to suggest matches.

Set up your wallet

Open the Wallet tab and create a wallet. Give it a name and a 4-digit wallet PIN. Zentra asks for that PIN whenever money leaves the wallet: withdrawals, gifts and the club fee. You can have up to three wallets once you've verified your identity. See Your wallet.

Add money

Tap Deposit and pick an amount. Zentra shows you a bank account number to send it to. Send exactly that amount within 10 minutes and your wallet is credited automatically when the money arrives. If you've verified your identity, you can also get a permanent account number to send money to at any time. See Deposits.

Play your first match

  • Quick Match: pick a game and a stake (₦200 or ₦500) and you'll be paired with the next player waiting. More
  • Accept an invite: if someone invites you, it shows in your Notifications. Open it to see the match and accept.
  • Load Match: type in a match code a friend sent you.
  • Create Match: set your own game, stake and players. More

If you're new to a game, read its rules page first: Chess, Whot, Lie, Ludo, Pool.

Before you stake

Decide what you can spend and stick to it. You can set a deposit limit and a reality-check reminder from Menu → Limits and breaks before you play your first match. See Limits, breaks and self-exclusion.
