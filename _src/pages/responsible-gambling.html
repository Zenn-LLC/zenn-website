Play for fun, and only with money you can afford to lose.

Keep it a game

  • Decide what you can spend before you play, and stop when it's gone.
  • Never chase a loss. The next match won't win it back for certain.
  • Don't play with money for rent, food, school fees or bills, and never borrow to play.
  • Take breaks. Don't play when you're upset, tired or have been drinking.
  • Winning isn't a way to make a living.

Signs it's becoming a problem

  • You stake more than you planned, or more to feel the same buzz.
  • You play to win back what you lost.
  • You've borrowed, sold things or skipped bills to play.
  • You hide how much you play from people close to you, or it causes arguments.
  • You feel restless or irritable when you try to stop.

If any of these sound like you, use the tools below and talk to someone.

Tools in the app

All of these are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Full details are on Limits, breaks and self-exclusion.

  • Deposit limits. Set the most you can deposit in any 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. A lower limit works at once. A higher one waits 24 hours.
  • Take a break. 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days or 30 days without matches, deposits or gifts.
  • Self-exclusion. 6 months, 1 year or 5 years. It can't be undone from the app.
  • Reality check. A reminder after every 30, 60 or 120 minutes of play, with how long you've played and how today is going.
  • Your money stays yours. Withdrawals still work during a break or self-exclusion.
  • Blocking. Block anyone who pressures you to play. See Blocking and reporting.

Under 18

Zentra is for adults only. We close any account of someone under 18. Parents can use the parental controls on their children's phones to keep them out of games with money.

Get help

Talking helps. These organisations are free and private:

A Nigerian helpline for anyone hurt by gambling: free, confidential, day and night.

Free counselling and mental health support in Nigeria.

Call 0809 111 6264 or 0811 168 0686 · mentallyaware.org

Gambling Therapy

Free, private online support and a forum, open to anyone in the world.

gamblingtherapy.org

Gamblers Anonymous

Meetings, including online ones, for people who want to stop gambling.
