Real money is staked on Zentra, so every game has to be won fairly. These are the rules, how Zentra checks them and what happens when someone breaks them.

Not allowed

  • Outside help during a game: chess engines, analysis apps or websites, or another person telling you what to play. That includes someone watching your game.
  • Teaming up: agreeing results with another player, losing on purpose, or passing money between accounts through games.
  • More than one account: one account per person, in your own name. No playing on someone else's account.
  • Using a fault: taking advantage of a bug in the app instead of reporting it.

How games are checked

  • Every move is recorded, and every real-money game between people is reviewed move by move, the same way as the game reviews you see.
  • Each game is compared with its own normal. In Ludo almost every move is close to the best, so high accuracy is ordinary there. In chess it isn't. A player is flagged only when their results stay far above what's normal for that game, over enough games that luck is a poor explanation.
  • Lie has its own check: someone who could see face-down cards would catch lies far more often than anyone else.
  • Clubmates who keep playing staked games with lopsided results are flagged.
  • Zentra's team can watch any live match without being shown as a watcher.

A flag is never a verdict on its own. It means a person at Zentra looks at the games.

What happens

If we find cheating, we may:

  • void results and keep or return stakes,
  • take back winnings from the games involved and return them to the players who lost them,
  • limit, suspend or close the accounts involved.

This is set out in section 8 of the Terms of Service.

Fair from Zentra's side

  • Cards are shuffled, dice rolled and Pool shots worked out on Zentra's servers, the same way for everyone.
  • Computer players are labelled, play within 25 points of your rating, and are never set up to make you lose.
  • Watchers only ever see what an opponent sees: never a hand or the deck.

Report it

If you think someone cheated in a game against you, raise it from the app: Home → Support → A problem with a match. Pick the match and tell us what happened. See Support and disputes.
