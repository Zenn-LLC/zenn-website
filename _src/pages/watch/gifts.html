When you're watching a live match, you can send one of its players a gift. It's a way to say well played. The player gets 90% at once, and Zentra keeps 10%.

Sending a gift

  1. Watch a live matchTap the gift button in the watching bar.
  2. Pick the player and the amount₦100, ₦500, ₦1,000 or ₦5,000.
  3. Pick the wallet and enter its PINMoney is leaving your wallet for someone else, so your 4-digit wallet PIN is needed every time.
  4. Everyone sees itThe players and everyone watching see a message like "Ada sent ₦500 to Tobi".

The player's share goes into their wallet straight away, and they can stake or withdraw it like any other money.

The rules

  • You have to be watching the match to send a gift in it.
  • You can't send a gift into your own match.
  • Computer players can't be sent gifts.
  • You can't send a gift to someone who has blocked you, or whom you've blocked.
  • You can send up to ₦50,000 in gifts a day.
  • Gifts can't be taken back.
  • You can't send gifts during a break or self-exclusion.

A gift is a present, not a stake. You get nothing back from it, whoever wins.
