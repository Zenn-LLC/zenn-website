Every Zentra Arena game is one on one, with a clock for each player and a winner at the end. There are no draws on Zentra Arena. Each game has a rules page and a tips page.

The games

Chess

Standard chess rules with a 15-minute clock each. Pre-moves and auto-queen are in the settings.

Rules · Tips

Whot

The Nigerian card game: 6 cards each, match the shape or number, and use your special cards well.

Rules · Tips

Lie

Zentra Arena's own bluffing game, played with Whot cards. Cards go down face down, and anyone can check.

Rules · Tips

Ludo

Two colours each and two dice. Roll a 6 to come out, capture to jump ahead, bring everyone home.

Rules · Tips

Pool

8-ball on a table the server simulates, so every shot plays out the same for both players.

Rules · Tips

What every game has

  • Your own clock. It only runs on your turn. When it reaches zero, you lose. See Clocks, resigning and rematches.
  • A resign button. Giving up ends the game at once and your opponent wins.
  • A rating for each game. Your Chess rating has nothing to do with your Ludo rating. See Ratings.
  • A review afterwards. Every move you made, labelled, with your accuracy. See Game reviews.
  • Settings that change only what you see, like board colours and sounds. See Game settings.
  • Match chat with your opponent, which you can turn off. See Match chat.

The server decides

Moves are checked and results are decided on Zentra Arena's servers, not on your phone. Cards are shuffled and dice are rolled there too, and in Pool the server works out where every ball goes. Your screen shows what the server decided, so both players always see the same game.
