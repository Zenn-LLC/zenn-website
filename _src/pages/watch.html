You can watch other people's games as they happen, react to the big moments and follow a match-up from game to game. Players choose who's allowed to watch them.

Finding a game to watch

  • From a friend's profile, while they're playing.
  • From the friends playing now row, which shows people you follow who are in a game.
  • From a tournament you're in: you can watch the other games in your tournament.

What you see

  • The game live: the board, the table or the cards on the table.
  • Only what an opponent would see. You never see a Whot or Lie hand, which cards can be played, or the deck.
  • The players see how many people are watching.
  • If none of the players are online, as can happen in a 24-hour match, you see a note saying so. Moves show up when they're back.

Reactions

Tap a reaction and everyone in the game sees it at once: haha, like, love, sad, or the review's own brilliant and blunder marks. Each watcher can send one a second.

Gifts

You can send a player you're watching a gift of ₦100, ₦500, ₦1,000 or ₦5,000. They get 90% straight away. See Gifts.

Following a match-up

At the end of each game in a match-up, there's a button that takes you straight to the next game, where you wait for the players.

Who can watch your games

Choose in the Menu, under Who can watch your games:

SettingWho can watch
Friends (the default)People you follow who follow you back.
FollowersAnyone who follows you.
No oneNobody, except players in the same tournament as you.
  • If anyone in a game has chosen No one, nobody outside the match can watch that game.
  • Someone you've blocked can never watch your games.

Zentra's team

To keep play fair, Zentra's team can watch any live match without being shown as a watcher. They can't react or send gifts. This is set out in the Terms of Service.

Coaching a player during a game, including from someone watching, is against the fair-play rules.
