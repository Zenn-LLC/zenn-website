About Zentra

What is Zentra?

A Nigerian platform for playing Chess, Whot, Lie, Ludo and Pool against real people, with a stake on each match. It runs in your browser at zentra.com.ng. See What is Zentra.

Who can play?

Adults aged 18 or over in Nigeria, with a Nigerian phone number. One account per person, in your own name.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Zentra runs in the browser on your phone or computer. See Play now.

Who runs Zentra?

Zenn Interactive Limited, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 9153066) and based in Lagos. See About Zenn.

Is it luck or skill?

Chess and Pool are skill. Whot, Lie and Ludo mix skill with the luck of the cards or dice. Over many games the better player wins more often, but any single match can go either way.

Money

How does Zentra make money?

It keeps 10% of what's staked in each match, 10% of each gift, and a one-off fee to start a club. When a computer player wins a match, the stake goes to Zentra. See Stakes and fees.

How much can I stake?

₦100 to ₦10,000 a player in challenges and tournaments, ₦200 or ₦500 in Quick Match, and ₦2,000 to ₦10,000 for a match-up.

What do I get if I win a ₦1,000 match?

In a ₦1,000 challenge, ₦2,000 is staked. The winner gets ₦1,800: their own ₦1,000 back plus ₦800. Zentra keeps ₦200.

How do I add money?

By bank transfer through Paystack, to a one-time account number or to your own permanent account number once you've verified your identity. See Deposits.

How do I withdraw, and how much?

To a bank account in your name, up to 3 times a day: ₦100 to ₦30,000 each time and ₦50,000 a day. Zentra pays the transfer fee. See Withdrawals.

Are there bonuses or free bets?

No. Zentra doesn't offer bonuses, free bets or deposit offers.

What if a match never starts?

You get your stake back in full. See Clocks, resigning and rematches.

Matches and games

Am I playing a real person?

In matches you create, tournaments, match-ups and club games: always. In one-on-one Quick Match (and a rematch after one), you may meet a computer player when nobody else is waiting. Its profile says Computer. See Computer players.

Are computer players set up to beat me?

No. Each one plays within 25 points of your rating, its strength never changes mid-match, and Zentra doesn't aim for any win rate. Against a player at your level, you should win about half the time.

Can a game end in a draw?

No. Every game has a winner. In chess, a drawn position goes to the player with more time left.

What if my internet drops?

Your clock keeps running on your turn. Rejoin as soon as you can. If your clock runs out, you lose. See Clocks, resigning and rematches.

Do I have to be online at the same time as my opponent?

Not for matches you create: each player gets 24 hours on their clock. Quick Match, match-ups and club games are played live.

Can people watch my games?

Only people you allow: by default, friends (people you follow who follow you back). You can change this to followers or no one. See Watching live.

Safety and your account

I forgot my PIN

Tap "Forgot your PIN?" on the sign-in screen. We'll text a code to your phone so you can set a new one.

Will Zentra ever ask for my PIN or a code?

Never. Don't share them with anyone. See Account security.

How do I take a break or stop playing?

Menu → Limits and breaks. You can set deposit limits, take a break of 24 hours to 30 days, or exclude yourself for 6 months, 1 year or 5 years. See Limits, breaks and self-exclusion.

Can I still withdraw during a break?

Yes. Your money stays yours, and withdrawals keep working.
