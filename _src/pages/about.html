What we believe

  • Skill should count. Ratings for every game, reviews of every move and fair-play checks on every real-money game between people.
  • The server decides. Moves, cards, dice and Pool shots are all settled on our servers, so both players see the same game and nobody can tamper with it from their phone.
  • Be straight about money. One fee, 10% of what's staked, shown before you join. No bonuses, no hidden charges, and withdrawals that work even during a break.
  • Be honest about computer players. They're labelled, they play at your level, and they're never set up to make you lose.
  • Look after players. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion that can't be undone on a whim, and help when it's needed.

Zentra

Zentra started with Ludo, Whot, Pool and Chess, games people in Nigeria grew up with, and added Lie, a bluffing game of our own. Around the games are clubs and leagues, match-ups, watching and gifts, reviews and achievements. Read What is Zentra, or see what's new on Updates.
