You can text your opponent during a match. Chat is between the players only, it's filtered to keep money and contact details out, and you can turn it down or off.

Using it

  • Tap the chat button in the game's bar to open the chat.
  • A message that arrives while the chat is closed shows as a one-line banner at the top of the game. Tap it to open the chat.
  • Only the players in the match can chat. People watching can't read or send messages.

What's filtered

  • Messages are up to 120 characters.
  • No links and no phone or account numbers (no runs of 7 or more digits).
  • Swear words are masked.
  • You can send a message every couple of seconds, up to 20 a minute.

Zentra Arena will never ask you to pay or move money through chat. If anyone offers you a deal outside the app, asks for money or asks for your details, don't reply. Report them.

Your chat setting

In the Menu you can choose how you chat in every match:

  • All: type anything.
  • Quick phrases: only Zentra Arena's set phrases.
  • Off: no chat at all.

You can also mute chat for a single match on your device.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report any message that breaks the rules. Reports go to Zentra Arena's team, who can delete messages and stop a player from using match chat.
  • If you've blocked someone, you don't see their messages. See Blocking and reporting.
  • Messages are kept for 30 days, so reports can be checked.

Computer players don't chat.
