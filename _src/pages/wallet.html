Your wallet holds the money you play with. You add money by bank transfer, stakes come out of it, winnings go into it, and you can withdraw to your own bank account.

Wallets and PINs

  • Create your first wallet from the Wallet tab. Give it a name and a 4-digit wallet PIN.
  • The wallet PIN is separate from the 6-digit PIN you sign in with. Zentra asks for it whenever money leaves the wallet for someone else or for your bank: withdrawals, gifts and the club fee.
  • You can have up to three wallets, for example one for small Quick Match stakes and one for tournaments. A second or third wallet needs a verified identity.
  • When you create or join a match, you pick which wallet the stake comes from.

What you see

  • Balance: what's available to stake or withdraw. Stakes in matches still being played aren't in it.
  • Activity: every stake, win, refund, deposit, withdrawal and gift, with filters for games, deposits and withdrawals. Tap a row for its details.
  • Wallet details: each wallet's name, its public key (which you can copy) and when you created it.

Your money is yours

  • Money in your wallet belongs to you. It earns no interest, and the wallet isn't a bank account.
  • You can withdraw at any time, including during a break or self-exclusion.
  • If you close your account, we pay your balance to your bank account first.
  • Zentra may hold a deposit or withdrawal while it checks it for fraud or a mistake, and will tell you if it does.

Money in and out

Deposits

Bank transfer through Paystack, to a one-time account or your own permanent account number.

How deposits work

Withdrawals

To your own bank account, up to 3 times a day, ₦100 to ₦30,000 each and ₦50,000 a day.
