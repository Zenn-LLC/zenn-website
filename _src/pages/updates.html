What's changed on Zentra Arena, newest first.

November 2026

Zentra Arena moved to the web, open to everyone in Nigeria at zentraarena.com, with a lot that's new:

  • Zentra Arena in your browser. The whole app runs on the web, on phones and computers, with nothing to install.
  • Lie, our own bluffing card game, joins Chess, Whot, Ludo and Pool. Rules
  • Clubs for each game, with members-only chat, club challenges and tournaments at set times, and leagues with divisions, promotion and trophies. Clubs
  • Match-ups: best-of-3 to best-of-11 series for one stake. Match-ups
  • 24-hour matches: matches you create give each player 24 hours on their clock. 24-hour matches
  • Watching live, with reactions, and gifts for players you're watching. Watching
  • Game reviews for all five games: every move labelled, with accuracy and coach notes. Reviews
  • Achievements for first wins, streaks, rating milestones and rare moments. Achievements
  • Computer players that play within 25 points of your rating, labelled Computer. Computer players
  • Match chat between players, filtered for links and numbers, with a setting to limit or turn it off. Match chat
  • Limits, breaks and self-exclusion, reality checks and two-way blocking. The tools
  • Support in the app for problems with a match or anything else. Support
  • This website, rebuilt with guides to all of it.

Before November 2026

Zentra Arena was in early access, with Ludo, Whot, Pool and Chess, while people joined the waiting list on this site.
