Pool on Zentra is 8-ball, one on one. Pot all the balls in your group, then pot the 8-ball to win.

The break

  • The balls are racked in a triangle with the 8-ball in the middle and one ball from each group in the back corners.
  • Any break that hits the rack is legal. The balls don't have to reach a cushion.
  • If the 8-ball goes in on the break, it comes back to the table and the breaker carries on. Potting the cue ball on the break is still a foul.

Choosing groups

After the break the table is open: neither player has a group yet. The first ball potted legally decides it. If you pot a solid, solids are yours and stripes are your opponent's. On an open table, hitting the 8-ball first is a foul.

Your turn

  • Hit one of your own balls first. If you pot one of your balls in a legal shot, you shoot again.
  • If you miss, or pot nothing, it's your opponent's turn.
  • Balls that go in stay down, even your opponent's. If you pot their ball, it counts for them.

Fouls

Any of these is a foul, and your opponent gets ball in hand: they can put the cue ball anywhere on the table before shooting.

  • Scratch: the cue ball goes in a pocket.
  • No contact: the cue ball hits nothing.
  • Wrong ball first: the cue ball hits one of your opponent's balls first (or the 8-ball first, before your group is cleared).
  • No cushion: after the cue ball hits a ball, nothing goes in and no ball touches a cushion.

The 8-ball

  • Once all your balls are down, pot the 8-ball legally to win.
  • Pot the 8-ball before your group is cleared and you lose.
  • Pot the 8-ball on a shot with a foul, such as scratching the cue ball, and you lose.

You also win if your opponent resigns or runs out of time.

Shooting on Zentra

  • Drag on the table to aim. On a phone, drag on the strip along the left side to set power, so one thumb holds power while the other aims.
  • Zentra's server works out every shot with the same physics for both players. Your screen plays back exactly what the server decided.
  • Aim sensitivity, cue, ball set and cloth colour are in Game settings.
