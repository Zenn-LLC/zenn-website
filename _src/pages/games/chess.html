Zentra plays chess by the standard rules: castling, en passant and promotion included. What's different is the clock, and what happens when a game would be drawn.

How a game ends

  • Checkmate: the player who checkmates wins.
  • Resigning: the player who resigns loses at once.
  • Running out of time: your clock runs on your turn only. If it reaches zero, you lose.
  • A drawn position goes to the clock. Stalemate, threefold repetition, the 50-move rule and positions where neither side can checkmate (insufficient material) all end the game. The player with more time left on their clock wins.

Because a draw is decided by the clock, time matters to the very end. If a game is heading for a draw, the player who has used less time wins it.

Moving on Zentra

  • Tap a piece, then tap a square, or drag the piece. Dots show where a piece can go (you can turn them off).
  • Pre-moves: you can queue a move during your opponent's turn. It's played as soon as it's your turn, if it's still legal.
  • Auto-queen: a pawn that reaches the last rank becomes a queen without asking. Turn it off to pick a knight, bishop or rook.
  • The board, pieces, coordinates and sounds can be changed in Game settings.

Long and short matches

Quick Match and rematches use the 15-minute clock, and both players are online at once. A match you create gives each player 24 hours on their clock instead, so you can make a move whenever you have a minute.

Fair play

Using a chess engine, a website, a book or another person's help during a game is cheating. Real-money games between people are reviewed for engine use. See Fair play.
