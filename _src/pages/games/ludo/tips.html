You can't choose your dice, but you choose what to do with them, and with two dice and eight tokens there's a lot to choose. These are the decisions that win Ludo games over time.

Captures come first

On Zentra a capture sends your token straight home and sends theirs back to its yard. That's a huge swing. Before moving, check whether either die, or the total, lands a token on one of theirs.

Think about both dice together

  • You can use the dice separately or as one total. Try the options: 4 and 3 could be two small moves, or one move of 7 that reaches a capture.
  • Use a 6 to bring a token out early in the game. More tokens on the board gives you more choices for later rolls.
  • Don't spend both dice running one token forward if it leaves others stuck in the yard.

Stay out of reach

  • Count the squares between your tokens and the opponent tokens behind them. Within a die's reach (1 to 6), or a likely total (up to 12), you can be captured.
  • When you can't be safe, prefer the square that needs a rarer roll to hit.
  • Once a token is in its home column, it's safe. Getting a token there can be worth more than moving one that's already far ahead.

Spread your tokens

  • Tokens spread round the board give you more ways to use each roll and more chances to capture.
  • Keep a token near the start to threaten opponent tokens that come round.

The end of the game

  • Finishing needs an exact count. Leave tokens at distances you can still roll, so small dice aren't wasted at the end.
  • If you're behind, keep tokens on the track to threaten captures rather than sitting them safe. If you're ahead, play safe.
