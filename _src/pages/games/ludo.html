Ludo on Zentra is one on one, and each player moves two colours with two dice. The first to bring all eight tokens home wins.

Setting up

One player has red and blue, the other has yellow and green. Each colour has four tokens that start in its home yard. Every token has to travel all the way round the board and up its own home column to the centre.

Your turn

  1. Roll both dice.
  2. Use each die on a token, one die at a time, on the same token or different ones. Or move one token by the total of both dice in one go.
  3. Your turn ends when you've used your dice, or when nothing you have can use what's left.

The rules that matter

  • A 6 brings a token out. A token in the yard can only come out on a die showing 6. The total of both dice doesn't count for this.
  • Double six rolls again. Roll two 6s and you get another roll once you've moved.
  • Exact count to finish. A token can't move past the centre. If a die would take it too far, you have to use that die somewhere else, or not at all.
  • No moves, no turn. If no token can use either die, your turn passes.

Capturing

Land a token exactly on an opponent's token and you capture it:

  • Their token goes back to its yard and needs a 6 to come out again.
  • Your token goes straight home to the centre. A capture is the fastest way to finish a token.

Your own two colours never capture each other. A token in its home column can't be captured, because no other colour goes there.

Winning

The first player to bring all eight tokens, both colours, home to the centre wins. You also win if your opponent resigns or runs out of time.

On Zentra

  • The dice are rolled on Zentra's server, never on your phone.
  • Move the only token: when every way of playing your roll ends in the same position, the game can make the move for you. You can turn this off.
  • Colour sets and sounds are in Game settings.
