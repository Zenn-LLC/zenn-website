Whot is the card game played across Nigeria. On Zentra it's one on one: be the first to play your last card.

The cards

The deck has five shapes: circles, triangles, crosses, squares and stars, each with its own set of numbers. There are also five Whot 20 cards.

Cross1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13, 14
Square1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13, 14
Star1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8
Whot20 (five of them)

Playing a turn

  • Each player is dealt 6 cards. One card is turned up to start the pile. The starting card is never a Whot.
  • On your turn, play one card that matches the top card's shape or its number. A Whot 20 can be played on anything, except when you're facing a Pick Two or Pick Three.
  • If you can't play, or don't want to, go to market: draw one card, and your turn ends.
  • When the market runs out, the cards already played (except the top one) are shuffled into a new market.

Special cards

CardNameWhat it does
1Hold OnYou play again.
2Pick TwoYour opponent draws 2, unless they play a 2 back. Then you draw 4, unless you play another 2, and so on.
5Pick ThreeYour opponent draws 3, unless they play a 5 back. Each 5 played back adds 3 more.
8SuspensionYour opponent misses a turn, so you play again.
14General MarketYour opponent draws 1 card, and you play again.
20WhotCall a shape. Your opponent must play that shape or a Whot. If they go to market instead, the call stays for the next player.

Only a 2 answers a Pick Two, and only a 5 answers a Pick Three. A Whot doesn't stop a pick-up. If you can't answer, going to market takes the whole pile of cards owed.

Winning

The first player to play their last card wins. You also win if your opponent resigns or runs out of time.

On Zentra

  • Cards you can play are lit up in your hand. You can sort your hand as dealt, by shape or by number.
  • Play the only card: when just one card can go (not a Whot), the game can play it for you. It's a setting you can turn off.
  • Card colours and card style (outline or filled) are in Game settings.
  • The deck is shuffled on Zentra's server, and you never see your opponent's hand.
