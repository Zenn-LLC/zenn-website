How to withdraw

  1. Tap WithdrawChoose the wallet and the amount.
  2. Choose the bank accountPick your bank and enter your account number. Zentra checks the account name with the bank before sending.
  3. Enter your wallet PINThe 4-digit PIN for that wallet. Tap to confirm.
  4. Wait for the transferIt shows as pending in your activity, then done. If a transfer fails, the money goes back to your wallet.

Whose account

  • Withdrawals only go to a bank account in your name.
  • Until you've verified your identity, it has to be the account that matches your phone number.

Withdrawals always work

You can withdraw during a break or self-exclusion. Your money stays yours. Zentra may hold a withdrawal while it checks it for fraud or a mistake, and will tell you if it does.

If something goes wrong

If a withdrawal shows as failed, check your balance: the money should be back in your wallet. If it says done but hasn't reached your bank, give your bank a little time, then contact support from the app with the date and amount. See Support and disputes.
