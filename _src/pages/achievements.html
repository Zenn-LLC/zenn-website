Achievements are badges for moments worth remembering: first wins, streaks, rating milestones, great moves and rare finishes. They show on your profile. None of them is for spending money or playing more hours, and none comes with a reward. They're just for you.

The rules

  • Nothing rewards stakes, deposits or the number of games you play in a day.
  • Games against computer players count.
  • Games you played before achievements arrived count too, where Zentra Arena has the record.
  • Once earned, a badge is yours to keep.
