Using the logo

  • Keep its shape and colours as they are.
  • Give it some space, on a dark or plain background.
  • Write the name as Zentra Arena, and the company as Zenn Interactive Limited.

Type

Headings use Space Grotesk, the typeface of the Zentra Arena app. Body text on this site uses DM Sans. Both are free from Google Fonts.

Describing Zentra Arena

A short description you can use:

Zentra Arena is a Nigerian platform for playing Chess, Whot, Lie, Ludo and Pool against real people, with a stake on each match. It runs in the browser at zentra.com.ng. 18+ only.

Please don't use the logo or name with promises of easy money, bonuses or guaranteed wins. Zentra Arena doesn't offer them. Always say it's for adults 18 and over, and link to Play responsibly.
