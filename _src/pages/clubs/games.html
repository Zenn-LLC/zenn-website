Inside a club you can talk with members and play them at times you agree. Club games use normal clocks and a set start time, so everyone's there together.

Club chat

  • Every club has a Chat tab that only members can see.
  • Messages are text only, up to 300 characters. No links.
  • You can send about one message every two seconds.
  • A dot shows when there are new messages. There's no notification for each message.
  • Messages are kept for 30 days.
  • The owner and admins can delete messages and mute a member for 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days.
  • Any member can report a message to Zentra's team.
  • If you've blocked someone, neither of you sees the other's messages.

Challenges between clubmates

  1. Challenge a clubmatePick the stake and a start time between 10 minutes and 7 days from now.
  2. They acceptBoth stakes are taken when the challenge is accepted.
  3. RemindersBoth players get a reminder 10 minutes before the start, and again at the start.
  4. Play at the start timeThe game uses the normal clock: 15 minutes each, 20 for Ludo.

No-shows: if you're not there within 5 minutes of the start and your opponent is, you lose. If neither player turns up, both stakes are returned.

Club tournaments

  • The owner or an admin sets one up for 4 or 8 members, with a stake and a start time.
  • Members join, and each stake is taken when you join.
  • If it isn't full at the start time, it's cancelled and everyone gets their stake back.
  • It runs like any tournament: knockout rounds, and the winner of the final takes the pot less Zentra's 10% fee.

Fair play inside clubs

Clubmates often play each other many times. Zentra's fair-play checks look for lopsided results in repeated staked games between the same clubmates, which can be a sign of money being passed between accounts. See Fair play.

Breaks and limits

During a break or self-exclusion you can't create, accept or join club games.
