Match-ups: best-of series on Zentra

A match-up is a best-of-3 to best-of-11 series between two players for one stake of at least ₦2,000.

On this page
  1. Setting one up
  2. Playing the series
  3. The money
  4. Ratings and reviews
  5. No draws
  6. Watching a match-up

A match-up settles who's better over a series, not one game. Two players play the same game best of 3, 5, 7, 9 or 11, for one stake, with the score on screen the whole way.

SeriesBest of 3–11odd numbers only
Stake₦2,000+each, for the whole series
DrawsNoneevery game has a winner

Setting one up

  1. Create Match → Match-upPick the game.
  2. Set the stakeAt least ₦2,000 and at most ₦10,000 each. It covers the whole series.
  3. Pick the lengthBest of 3, 5, 7, 9 or 11.
  4. Invite your opponentMatch-ups are always invite only. You can also set a start time.

Playing the series

  • Match-ups are live. Both players are online and tap Ready before each game, and games use the normal clock (15 minutes, Ludo 20).
  • The first move alternates from game to game.
  • The series score is on screen throughout.
  • The series stops as soon as someone can't be caught. In a best of 5, the first to win 3 games wins, and the rest aren't played.
  • If one player is ready and the other doesn't turn up within 10 minutes, the player who didn't show loses that game, and the series moves on.
  • If neither player starts the first game within 30 minutes, the match-up is cancelled and both stakes are returned.

The money

Each player stakes once, at the start. The series winner gets both stakes less Zentra's 10% fee, paid when the series is decided. For example, at ₦5,000 each, ₦10,000 is staked and the series winner gets ₦9,000.

Ratings and reviews

Every game in a match-up is rated like a normal game, so a best of 7 can move your rating seven times. Every game also gets its own review.

No draws

Every Zentra game ends with a winner, so every game counts towards the series. In chess, a drawn position goes to the player with more time left on their clock.

Watching a match-up

People watching can follow the whole series. At the end of each game there's a button that takes them straight to the next one, where they wait for the players. See Watching live.

18+

Zentra is played for real money and is only for adults in Nigeria. Stake only what you can afford to lose. Limits, breaks and self-exclusion are in the app under Menu → Limits and breaks. Play responsibly
